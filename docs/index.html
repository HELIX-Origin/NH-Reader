About

NH Reader is a native desktop and Android client for nhentai.net, not a browser-based reader. Browse with powerful search and filters, organize your library, and apply a persistent global blacklist. Your favorites, history, and settings are stored locally.

18+ only. This software is intended for adults who are of legal age to view adult content in their jurisdiction. NH Reader is an independent client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by nhentai.net.

Highlights & Features

  • 🔍 Search & filter

    Find galleries by language, category, tags, page count, and sort order.

  • 🛡️ Global blacklist

    Exclude unwanted tags from searches and hide or blur matches in discovery grids.

  • 📚 Local library

    Keep favorites and history on your device, download archives, and read them offline.
